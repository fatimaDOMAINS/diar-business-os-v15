DIAR · PRIVATE V11 PILOT
BOCOBITES × DIAR
Prepared for Fleur Anderson · BOCOBITES

Turn BOCOBITES demand into a scalable B2B growth engine.

A brand-first digital business concept designed to identify intent, qualify opportunities and route the right retail, hospitality, corporate gifting and distribution conversations into one measurable pipeline.

Private concept for validation. It does not assume future revenue, conversion rates, production capacity, inventory availability or agreed commercial terms.

What already exists

BOCOBITES has a differentiated chef-founded product, an established brand story and clear B2B use cases. The pilot does not replace that brand.

What DIAR adds

Intent capture, qualification, CRM workflow, follow-up, opportunity tracking and conversion analytics around the existing business.

The objective

Turn attention and relationships into structured, measurable commercial conversations without forcing every visitor through the same funnel.

DOMAIN DECISION

BOCOBITES stays first.

Umami365.com is optional — not the pitch.

The pilot keeps BOCOBITES as the customer-facing brand. Umami365.com only becomes relevant later if a broader premium-food discovery or distribution platform proves strategically useful.

PROPOSED NEXT STEP

Validate one B2B lane before discussing a larger JV.

Choose one lane — for example retail/wholesale or corporate gifting — connect it to a small real campaign, measure qualified conversations, then decide whether a deeper DIAR × BOCOBITES partnership creates enough value to expand.
